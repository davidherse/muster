# Muster — Product Direction (reset 2026-07)

## Who it's for

Estimators — inside a residential building company, or offering estimating as a
service. Not a replacement for real quotes; a way to get to a defensible number
faster.

## The job to be done

Give a lead a **ballpark before spending real money estimating**. Hundreds of
dollars (and days) go into estimating leads that go nowhere. Muster produces a
ballpark that is defensible within **±10% either way**, good enough to put in
front of a lead before they indicate they want to proceed. When third-party
prices come back, the estimate is updated with real numbers.

## Order of accuracy work

1. **Quantities and breakdown first, without cost.** If the line items,
   structure, and quantities are right, prices can be layered on however we
   like. This is the current focus: measure and improve line-item coverage and
   quantity accuracy, ignoring price accuracy.
2. **Unit prices second.** Sourced in this order:
   a. Industry market averages for the location.
   b. The builder's own historical data, adjusted for inflation.

## What "training" means now

Every builder structures estimates differently — sections, granularity, what
gets itemised vs allowed. Uploaded examples are used for two things:

1. **Structure** — learn the template: sections, ordering, common line items,
   level of breakdown, formatting. This is the primary purpose.
2. **Price ranges** — a benchmark of common unit-price ranges, used as a sanity
   band, not a bias adjustment.

Explicitly NOT: calibration bias applied to totals, dual-mode estimate data.

## Onboarding flow

1. User signs up (invite-gated beta).
2. Wizard: upload example estimates/costings (spreadsheet or CSV) →
   review and agree the derived estimate template → uploads are broken down
   into the price book for later.
3. User creates estimates: questionnaire + plans → line items with quantities
   (prices present but secondary for now).

## Evaluation

Primary metric: **structure + quantity accuracy** against real human
estimates — section/line-item coverage and quantity error — not total price
error. Price benchmark (±8% on totals) is retired as the primary gate until
phase 2.
